## 2026-11-07 — Windowed `{#for}` lists

`{#for item in items key={…} window=48 overscan=4}` builds only the items near the scroll viewport plus two spacer boxes sized `skipped × window` along the parent's main axis, so scroll extent is unchanged. ## 2026-11-08 — Pointer hit tests prune subtrees by painted extent

The pointer traversals (`pointer_hit_test`, `pointer_press_hit`, `pointer_event_handler_hit`) visited every row of a fully built list. They now take an optional `PaintedExtents` oracle (`pointer_*_pruned`) and skip a child whose subtree painted nothing at the point. The oracle is the retained display list: `RetainedDisplayList::painted_subtree_bounds` is each node's `command_span.bounds`, which already includes ancestor transforms, effect overflow and clips, and `is_retained_scroll_owner` marks owners whose content bounds are unscrolled content space, so the traversal adds the live scroll offset of each such owner above a node before comparing. Rules that keep it sound: a node without a recorded extent (hidden, preclipped, new, never built) is never pruned; a scrolling node that does not replay its content disables pruning below it, because its build-time offset is baked into the bounds; bounds get a 1 px slack for the display list's integer rounding; and the component supplies the oracle only while nothing is pending (`dirty`, `style_only_dirty`, `animation_only_dirty`, `next_frame_only_dirty`, `dirty_types`, `frame_dirty`), since a pending change can move or add nodes the list has not seen. Wired into pointer motion, press, the press-target lookup, scroll, two-finger scroll and gesture capture. Tests: interaction unit tests (pruned equals full, a shrunk extent is skipped, unknown nodes are never pruned, retained-owner shift, non-retained scroller disables pruning) and `painted_extent_pruning_matches_the_full_hit_test_on_a_scrolled_uneven_list` (300 uneven rows, scrolled to the top, middle and end; hit, press and handler results equal the full traversal over a point grid; a hit visits 5–14 nodes). interaction 55, frontend-runtime 744, render 377 pass; `cold_icon_lookup_repaints_only_consumers_and_rejects_old_catalogs` fails with and without this change. Replay pruning for the same lists is still open in the backlog.

## 2026-11-08 — Replay skips scroll content outside the viewport

A retained scroll owner's push/pop is a layer scope, so selection always replayed its whole content and the painter rejected rows one at a time. `RetainedDisplayList` now builds, once per frame plan, an index of each top-level scope's content as `(start, end, bounds)` entries (a nested scope is one entry), and `skip_offscreen_scroll_content` drops the entries whose content-space bounds miss the push command's viewport shifted by the owner's scroll, with 1 px slack. The push, the pop and everything outside the scope stay, so layers remain balanced; full-surface selection now becomes spans when such a scope exists (`select_all`). Test: `full_surface_replay_skips_content_outside_the_scrolled_viewport` (40 rows at four offsets: fewer than 20 commands replayed, every on-screen row present, push and pop present). render, frontend-runtime and interaction suites pass.

## 2026-11-08 — Uneven `{#for}` lists window against measured and estimated pitches

A long list whose items differ in size was built in full (a ~300 ms second frame at 2,000 rows). `VirtualRange` gained `spacers: Option<(lead, trail)>`; the compiler uses them in place of `skipped * (extent + gap)` when present. The host (`virtual_list.rs`) keeps a `PitchCache` per list: when the built items are not uniform, or a recorded extent no longer matches, the list enters variable mode (sticky until its length changes), records each built item's pitch (to the next item or trailing spacer, gap included), fixes an estimate for unbuilt items (mean of the known pitches when it enters), and chooses the window from cumulative pitches. Spacer sizes are sums of pitches minus one gap; a rebuild is requested when the recorded spacer sizes or estimate differ from the computed ones, so a settled list asks for no change. Lists without a scroller still build in full. Tests: variable estimate and spacers, mismatched recorded extent, a settled list is stable, scrolling uses cumulative pitches, and `large_plain_list_with_uneven_rows_is_windowed_and_scrolls_to_the_end` (2,000 rows, every 7th 55 px, fewer than 100 built, reaches row 2000). frontend-runtime 746 and frontend pass; `tray_provider_registers_updates_activates_and_reuses_an_existing_watcher` fails in this environment (private D-Bus) and is unrelated. Open: scroll anchoring when estimates are replaced by measurements.
